Rope Braided Flexible Connectors

Central Tool Specialties manufactures rope braided flexible connectors to customer specifications and drawings. Ideal for connections with limited space availability, these connectors excel in horizontal flexibility applications, flexing equally in all directions. They are an industry standard for damping vibrations in complex electrical machinery.

Assembly & Integration

Typically used in welding and power equipment, and for electrical components such as switches, robots, and high-motion assembly line equipment. Their omni-directional flex makes them ideal for vibration-heavy environments.
