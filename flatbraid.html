Flat Braided Flexible Connectors

Central Tool Specialties manufactures flat braided flexible connectors to customer specifications and drawings for applications including expansion joints and connections for electrical and bus duct equipment. Ideal for damping vibrations from generators and transformers, these connectors align equipment and reduce energy loss in high-motion environments.

Series Classification

Grounding & Power Assembly

  • Electrical Bus Ducts
  • Battery Racks/Packs
  • Transformer Boxes
  • Vibration Damping for Generators

Ferrules: Tin (T), Nickel (N), Silver (S), or Bare (B).
Braided Material: Tin (T) or Bare (B).
