Copper bus bars & OEM Metals

Central Tool Specialties manufactures high-quality copper and aluminum bus bars and OEM parts tailored to precise customer specifications and technical drawings. By allowing circuits to branch off anywhere along the route, our bus bar solutions provide superior flexibility compared to traditional localized supply points.

While we work with various materials, copper remains the preferred choice for industrial busways due to its optimal electrical conductivity and natural resistance to corrosion.

Our bus bar components are engineered for critical infrastructure, including:
  • Grounding Bars & Bus Ducts
  • Control Switches & Switchgear
  • UPS Systems
  • Battery Racks and Packs
  • Custom OEM Metal Assemblies
To meet specific environmental or conductivity requirements, we offer a full suite of professional plating options:
  • Tin Plating (Corrosion resistance)
  • Nickel Plating (Hardness and durability)
  • Silver Plating (Maximum surface conductivity)
  • Bare Copper or Aluminum finishes
Copper bus bars offer superior thermal expansion properties and higher conductivity per cross-sectional area compared to aluminum. This allows for more compact designs in switchgear and power distribution centers without sacrificing performance or safety.
