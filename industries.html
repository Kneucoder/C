Mitigating Operational Risk

Transformers, generators, switches, and turbines are the heart of power plants and substations. However, the constant vibrations caused by this heavy equipment pose a significant threat to electrical integrity.

Left unmanaged, these vibrations result in loose connections across bolts, lugs, and bus bars—leading to performance degradation, equipment failure, and a heightened risk of fire.

!

Vibration is the primary cause of loose connections in electrical grids.
